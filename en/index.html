About me

Hi, I'm Zakary.

I hold an Honours B.Sc. in Computer Science from Université de Montréal, with a cumulative GPA of 3.89/4.3 and the excellence distinction in 7 of 8 terms. I'm now taking graduate courses in operations research.

I've been teaching since 2019: as a tutor at Cégep du Vieux-Montréal, as a video game design instructor at StudioXP for youth aged 8 to 18, and as a teaching assistant at UdeM.

My approach: I'm patient, I explain things step by step and I adapt to each student's level. I focus on understanding rather than memorizing, so students walk into their exams with confidence.

  • Honours B.Sc. in Computer Science, Université de Montréal (2021–2025): A+ in algorithms, data structures, linear optimization, software engineering and databases.
  • Graduate coursework in operations research, UdeM (2025–2026): A in operations research methods and integer programming.
  • Teaching assistant at UdeM: IFT1025 Programming 2 (Java, Fall 2025) and IFT2015 Data Structures (Winter 2026).
  • Tutor at Cégep du Vieux-Montréal (2019–2020): differential calculus and cell biology.
  • Video game design instructor at StudioXP (since 2022), teaching youth aged 8 to 18.
  • Full-stack developer (React/Java) of a research web platform, co-author of a paper in Genome Biology (2026) and recipient of an NSERC research award (2025).
  • Bilingual French and English, conversational Spanish.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do in-person sessions take place?

At the student's home or in a public place in Montréal, such as a library or café. There's no travel fee within 45 minutes of Préfontaine metro station by public transit. If you're farther away, get in touch and we'll find a solution.

Can you do my assignments?

No. I explain and guide, but I don't do graded work for you. I can, however, help you understand the material and the method so you can succeed on your own.

How do questions by message work?

With a package, you can send me short questions by text, WhatsApp or Discord: a photo of an exercise, a bug, a concept. I reply within 24 h, between 9 am and 9 pm (within 12 h with the exam package). Longer questions are moved to the next session.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer. Packages are paid at the start of the month and single sessions after each session.

Can a parent attend the session?

Yes, of course. Parents are welcome at the free meeting and at sessions, especially with younger kids.

What if I need to cancel?

Let me know at least 24 h in advance and we'll reschedule. Cancellations with less than 24 h notice are charged.

Do you help with exam prep?

Yes. The exam package includes 2 sessions of 2 h within 2 weeks and priority questions (reply within 12 h) until the exam. For high school, I also prepare students for ministry exams.

Do you tutor in English?

Yes. I'm bilingual: sessions can be in French or English, whichever you prefer.
